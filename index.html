Ink.

A drop of ink meets still water. What follows is a small, slow turbulence: the drop sinks, curls into itself, thins into filaments, and dissolves.

Everything here is computed on the GPU in real time: an incompressible fluid solver (semi-Lagrangian advection, a Jacobi pressure solve, vorticity confinement) carries the ink, gravity pulls it downward, and a diffusion term softens its edges. In paper mode the ink is rendered as pigment absorbing light; in water mode it glows as if lit from the side.
